I was down at the station  
I was idling in line  
Engine off and windows down  
I was thinking about stillness and motion  
It was evening time

Rolling over the water  
I heard that lonesome tune  
Before the lights of the ferry  
Had even come into view

I’ve been thinking about the journey  
The wind that cuts you clean in two  
Currents grasping at the tiller  
Rocks lurking out of view

I know that ferryman  
Will get us all safely there  
Humming to himself  
There is no other harm to bear
